# Studio Inspirasi 0.1.12

Desktop release of the Studio Inspirasi fork for macOS Monterey 12 (Intel) and the platforms Clovercut already ships.

## Animate: entrance, exit and loop

The old Animation panel's four presets are replaced by Clovercut's Animate panel, brought over from upstream.

- **14 entrances and exits:** Fade, Slide Left, Slide Right, Slide Up, Slide Down, Zoom In, Zoom Out, Pop, Drop, Rise, Spin, Flip, Swing and Squash.
- **9 loops** that play for the whole clip: Pulse, Heartbeat, Float, Wiggle, Sway, Jitter, Spin, Flicker and Breathe.
- Every card plays its own move while the pointer is on it or while it is picked, so you see what the clip will do before choosing.
- Each entrance and exit has its own length; a loop has a lap length and an amount.
- Works on video, pictures, titles, captions and shapes. The move rides over the clip's own keyframes instead of replacing them, and it is in the preview and the export alike.
- Find it under **Video → Animate** for pictures and shapes, and **Text → Animate** for titles and captions. With "apply to all" on, a caption's animation goes to its whole collection.

## Compatibility

- Projects saved with the old Fade, Slide or Pop presets open and play as before. Picking a new move for that phase replaces the old preset, in one undo step.
- The separate caption Animation tab is gone; its place is the Animate section of the Text tab.

All features from 0.1.11 remain included. Mobile packages are not part of this release.
